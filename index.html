about

The olympiad taught me how to think; programming is how I relax. TestBox

offline-first study & test companion — web · windows · android

An app I built to manage practice tests: organize exams into folders and subjects, solve sheets with an exam timer or practice stopwatch, track daily study on a calendar, and review marked questions. Everything works offline and syncs when you're back. Built for my own konkur prep — and it shows: I use it every day.

  • Exam mode with negative marking & practice stopwatch
  • Daily reports, streaks & monthly activity calendar (Jalali & Gregorian)
  • Offline-first sync — upload on reconnect, no lost edits
  • Full FA (RTL) & EN, light & dark themes
