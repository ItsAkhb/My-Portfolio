01about

I'm a 12th grader at Hasheminejad High School in Mashhad, spending this year preparing for the konkur on the math–physics track. Before that, I spent a few good years deep in C++ and algorithms as a Computer Olympiad trainee — including competing in Round 2 of the national olympiad, where my run ended.

The olympiad taught me how to think; programming is how I relax. I still write code for the fun of it — small tools built to learn something new, like TestBox, an offline-first study app I actually use myself. Off the screen: volleyball court, good playlists, and a never-ending debate over what to order at the café.

The playlists run heavy. I'm a metalhead, mostly tech death for its precision and density and funeral doom for its patience — two ends of the same instinct that pulled me into algorithms: build something with structure, then let it breathe.

TestBox · study session
v1.0.0 · offline

exam mode · stopwatch

An app I built to manage practice tests: organize exams into folders and subjects, solve sheets with an exam timer or practice stopwatch, track daily study on a calendar, and review marked questions. Everything works offline and syncs when you're back. Built for my own konkur prep — and it shows: I use it every day.

  • Exam mode with negative marking & practice stopwatch
  • Daily reports, streaks & monthly activity calendar (Jalali & Gregorian)
  • Offline-first sync — upload on reconnect, no lost edits
